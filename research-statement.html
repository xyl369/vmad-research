Research question and intended contribution

研究问题与拟新增贡献

VMAD asks whether quantum degrees of freedom, entanglement and dynamics can support effective spacetime and matter, whether a geometric phase can change locally, and what physical conditions would permit metric control. The proposed chain is quantum state and dynamics → effective geometry → local geometric change → admissible control → observable response. Its connections remain to be established.

Here, local loss of geometry means failure of a specified effective geometric description. A physical phase transition requires additional dynamics and scale-dependent evidence; reconstruction failure or ordinary decoherence alone is insufficient. Matter emergence is a long-term question: the current models input spin or scalar degrees of freedom and do not derive the observed particle spectrum.

The six-month study will fix one tractable published quantum model and reconstruction map. It will test when an admissible local change preserves, alters or invalidates that model's geometric description, controlling for partition choice, coarse-graining, missing information and numerical error. Entropy-based spatial reconstruction is the first candidate route; the model and precise claim will be fixed in month one.

The intended contribution is a model-specific validity condition, counterexample or precisely located obstruction, assessed against prior work. Progress toward physical metric control additionally requires dynamics, matter coupling, conservation, causality and resource accounting. Existing scalar-gravity calculations supply a conventional comparison, not the proposed emergence mechanism.

VMAD研究量子自由度、纠缠和动力学能否支持有效时空及物质，几何相能否发生局部变化，以及什么物理条件允许度规控制。拟议链条是：量子态与动力学→有效几何→局部几何变化→允许的控制→可观测响应。这些连接仍需建立。

这里的局部几何失效，指某个明确有效几何描述不再适用。物理相变另需动力学与尺度相关证据；重建失败或普通退相干本身不足以成立。物质涌现是长期问题：当前模型输入自旋或标量自由度，尚未推导现实粒子谱。

六个月将固定一个可处理的已发表量子模型及重建映射，检验一种允许的局部变化在什么条件下保持、改变或使该模型的几何描述失效，并控制分区、粗粒化、信息不足与数值误差。基于熵的空间重建是首选候选路线，具体模型和命题在第一个月固定。

拟取得的贡献是限定模型的有效条件、反例或准确定位的阻碍，并与前人工作对照。走向物理度规控制还需动力学、物质耦合、守恒、因果与资源核算。现有标量—引力计算提供常规物理对照，不代表拟议涌现机制。

The complete research architecture

完整研究架构

VMAD (Vacuum Metastability & Anomalous Domain) is the working name of Xiaoyi Li's independent, non-commercial theoretical research programme. Its central ambition is to investigate whether quantum states and dynamics can yield effective spacetime and matter, whether a geometric regime can change locally, and what additional conditions would make any resulting geometric response physically controllable. The expanded name identifies the project; it does not assert a demonstrated false-vacuum decay mechanism.

The complete architecture extends from microscopic physics to localized structures, measurements, resource budgets and possible long-term systems. Each connection has a different evidential requirement. The immediate research programme isolates one tractable quantum model and reconstruction map, while keeping the wider architecture available as a set of explicit questions rather than treating every connection as an established result.

VMAD（Vacuum Metastability & Anomalous Domain）是李小毅独立、非商业理论研究项目的工作名称。核心目标是研究：量子态与动力学能否产生有效时空及物质；几何状态能否发生局部转变；以及还需要哪些条件，才可能使相应几何响应受到物理控制。英文全称用于标识项目，不表示已经证明了某种假真空衰变机制。

完整架构从微观物理延伸到局域结构、测量、资源核算与可能的长期系统。不同连接需要不同证据。近期研究先限定一个可处理的量子模型和几何重建映射，同时把较长的架构保留为一组明确问题，逐项推进。

1. Microscopic quantum states and dynamics

1. 微观量子态与动力学

The starting question is what the underlying physical system actually is. A candidate must specify degrees of freedom, state space, observable algebra, subsystem factorization or constraints, dynamics, and an admissible family of states. Entanglement is meaningful only relative to that specification. If time itself is to emerge, an externally supplied time parameter must be identified as an assumption rather than quietly counted as a result.

Preparation currently includes finite Ising-state diagnostics and a literature comparison of distinct microscopic approaches. Nine eight-qubit cases and 1,143 bipartitions have undergone AI-assisted cross-implementation checks. These calculations take the graph and quantum degrees of freedom as inputs. The next task is to select one published model and record its complete specification, approximation and applicant-reviewed baseline.

起点是明确“底层系统究竟是什么”。候选模型必须给出自由度、态空间、可观测量代数、子系统划分或约束、动力学和允许的态族。纠缠只有在这些规格确定之后才有明确含义。如果时间本身也待涌现，外加的时间参数必须列为输入前提，不能算成已经得到的结果。

当前准备包括有限Ising态诊断及不同微观路线的文献比较。九组八量子比特算例、1,143个二分割已做AI辅助的不同实现交叉检查；图结构和自由度仍是给定输入。下一步是固定一个已发表模型，完整记录规格、近似与本人审阅的基线。

2. From quantum relations to spacetime and matter

2. 从量子关系到时空与物质

A reconstruction dictionary must explain which quantum data correspond to distance, adjacency, dimensionality or other geometric observables, and when that dictionary is reliable. Spatial reconstruction is an initial step. Recovering causal propagation, effective field equations and a common coupling to matter is a stronger requirement. Different holographic, matrix, condensate and other emergence approaches cannot be combined simply by taking the most favourable conclusion from each.

The framework organizes these interfaces, and the finite calculations examine selected entropy relations and prescribed coarse partitions. They do not yet reproduce a complete spatial-reconstruction theorem or derive observed particle species. The next step is to reproduce one relevant published reconstruction result and state its factorization, information and scale assumptions before investigating allowed changes of state.

重建字典要说明：哪些量子数据对应距离、邻接、维数或其他几何量，以及字典在什么条件下可靠。重建空间只是第一步；恢复因果传播、有效场方程及对物质的共同耦合，是更强的要求。全息、矩阵、凝聚态等不同涌现路线具有不同输入，不能把各自最有利的结论直接拼成一套理论。

现有框架整理了这些接口，有限计算检查部分熵关系与指定粗分块。它们尚未完整复现空间重建结果，也未导出现实粒子种类。下一步先复现一个相关已发表结果，列清分解、信息与尺度假设，再研究模型允许的态变化。

3. Local geometric transitions and loss of description

3. 局部几何相变与描述失效

The proposed local-transition question asks whether one physical regime can cease to admit its previous geometric description. A failed numerical embedding, insufficient correlation data and an actual phase transition are different possibilities. A physical transition needs dynamics, a suitable diagnostic or order parameter, controlled size or continuum behaviour, and a transition path. A local region additionally needs matching to its surroundings and an account of what persists across the interface.

Current work has identified these requirements but has not supplied a VMAD transition law or demonstrated physical spacetime dissolution. The near-term test applies one model-allowed local change and separates genuine changes in the chosen geometric criterion from partition dependence, noise, missing information and finite-size error. Failure criteria are part of the expected result.

局部转变问题考察：某个物理状态是否会不再适用原有的几何描述。数值嵌入失败、关联数据不足和真实物理相变是不同情况。物理相变需要动力学、合适诊断量或序参量、受控的尺寸或连续极限，以及转变路径；局部区域还需要说明与周围的匹配条件及界面两侧保持什么。

目前已整理这些必要条件，尚未给出VMAD转变定律，也未证明真实时空的局部消亡。近期检验将在模型允许范围内施加一种局部变化，区分选定几何判据的物理变化与分区、噪声、缺失信息和有限尺寸误差。失败条件同样属于预期成果。

4. Relativistic consistency and effective gravity

4. 相对论一致性与有效引力

An effective metric must support mutually compatible clocks, propagation and freely moving probes. The model must identify its gravitational degrees of freedom, coupling assumptions, causal domain, constraints and regime of validity. Writing a desired metric and calculating the stress-energy it would require does not establish that an admissible source can produce it. Quantum-source backreaction and stress fluctuations matter when a semiclassical approximation is used.

The present manuscripts set out these consistency tests and use established weak-field gravity for comparison. The scalar-gravity benchmark is separate from the quantum reconstruction programme: microscopic matching between them has not been derived. The next theoretical audit will identify exactly which causal and matter-coupling assumptions the selected reconstruction route supplies, and which remain additional requirements.

有效度规应当同时支持相容的时钟、传播和自由探针运动。模型需要明确引力自由度、耦合前提、因果适用域、约束和有效范围。先写一个想要的度规，再算出所需应力能，不等于证明存在可实现的源。使用半经典近似时，还需要检查量子源反作用和应力涨落。

已有稿件整理了这些一致性检验，并以成熟弱场引力作对照。标量—引力基准与量子重建属于不同计算，尚未推导二者的微观匹配。下一步将审核所选重建路线到底提供了哪些因果和物质耦合条件，哪些仍需另加。

5. Localized structures, formation and stability

5. 局域结构、形成与稳定性

A localized object requires more than an attractive drawing or the label “soliton”. Its model must admit a finite-energy configuration with specified boundaries, a formation route, perturbative behaviour, lifetime and response to disturbances. Conserved charge, topology, time dependence or gravity may stabilize different systems; none automatically establishes every required property. Mass, inertia and environmental response must follow from the same physical description.

A normalized, nodeless Schrödinger-Poisson reference and a static quadrupole response have been checked with domain, mesh and virial controls. They concern a known weak-field scalar-gravity approximation. A norm-preserving scaling test is not complete stability analysis. Further work requires a perturbation spectrum, nonlinear evolution and a formation mechanism before this comparison can support claims about a lasting, usable object.

局域对象不能只靠形状图或“孤子”名称成立。模型要给出有限能构型、边界、形成路径、扰动行为、寿命及外界干扰响应。守恒荷、拓扑、时间依赖或引力可能稳定不同系统，但任何一项都不会自动保证全部性质。质量、惯性与环境响应应来自同一物理描述。

当前已对归一、无节点的Schrödinger–Poisson参考态及静态四极响应进行截断、网格与virial检查，适用对象是已知弱场标量—引力近似。保持归一的缩放检验不是完整稳定性证明。后续需要扰动谱、非线性演化和形成机制，才能进一步讨论持久可用对象。

6. Preparation, control and recovery

6. 制备、控制与恢复

Existence of a mathematical state does not imply experimental reachability. Metric engineering would require a specified physical intervention, permitted interactions, preparation fidelity, bandwidth, response time and full controller accounting. The apparatus can introduce ordinary forces, electromagnetic fields, heat and noise. A change in logical correlations does not by itself keep the whole apparatus's stress-energy unchanged.

The original framework gives a four-qubit pairing example in which single-node reduced states stay the same while joint correlations differ. This is a mathematical control concept; the proposed metrology experiment has not been performed and no complete nonzero geometric response law is supplied. Next steps are an explicit permitted operation, a candidate response template, sham switching and calibrated ordinary cross-talk controls.

数学态存在，不等于实验可以到达。度规工程需要明确的物理干预、允许相互作用、制备保真度、带宽、响应时间及整个控制器的核算。装置本身可能产生普通力、电磁场、热和噪声。逻辑关联改变，并不能保证整个设备的应力能保持不变。

原框架给出四量子比特换配对算例：单节点约化态相同，联合关联不同。这是数学控制构想；相应计量实验尚未实施，也没有完整的非零几何响应定律。下一步应明确允许操作、候选响应模板、伪开关以及普通串扰标定。

7. A common source and multiple observables

7. 共同物理源与多通道观测

The strongest operational connection in the framework is that one source must generate mutually constrained signals. In a stated static weak-field regime, clock differences probe potential differences, free fall probes gradients, tides probe further derivatives, and light propagation supplies complementary constraints. Source parameters cannot be adjusted independently for each channel. Complete accounting includes the driving apparatus's direct field as well as the induced response.

Existing calculations illustrate clock and tidal predictions, direct-versus-induced contributions and ordinary-source degeneracy. Equal Newtonian density profiles can give equal predictions despite different microscopic interpretations. Next steps are a fully specified forward model and a held-out channel: fit limited data, freeze parameters, and predict another measurement with its calibration and uncertainty.

框架中最直接的可操作连接是：同一物理源必须产生相互约束的信号。在明确的静态弱场条件下，钟差读出势差，自由落体读出梯度，潮汐读出进一步导数，光传播提供补充约束。不能为每个通道独立调整源参数。总账还应同时包含驱动装置直接场和诱导响应。

已有计算示范钟差与潮汐预测、直接与诱导贡献，以及普通源简并：不同微观解释若给出相同Newton密度，也可能给出相同观测。下一步是固定完整正演模型，先用部分数据估参，再冻结参数预测另一个通道，并计入标定和不确定度。

8. Motion, media, appearance and shape

8. 运动、介质、外观与形状

Motion must account for momentum exchange, surrounding fluid, scattering, radiation and the instrument that produces the record. Zero proper acceleration at one point does not imply zero tidal stress. No visible exhaust does not remove momentum or energy requirements. A sphere, capsule, faceted body or X-shaped surface pattern can reflect different dynamics, material properties, propagation effects and viewing conditions.

The manuscripts contain ordinary-medium cost estimates, geometric shape comparisons and limits on interpreting distant surface patterns from deep sources. They have not produced a self-consistent accelerated VMAD solution or reconstructed an extreme trajectory from complete event data. The next task is a source-to-medium-to-sensor calculation with defined observer, distance, scale, uncertainty and competing ordinary mechanisms.

运动需要同时核算动量交换、周围流体、散射、辐射及生成记录的仪器。某点固有加速度为零，不意味着潮汐应力为零；没有可见喷流，也不会免除能量动量要求。球形、胶囊、分面或海面X形可能分别来自动力学、材料、传播和观察条件。

已有稿件提供普通介质代价估算、几何形状比较，以及由深源解释远处表面细节的限制。尚未得到自洽的加速VMAD解，也未从完整事件数据重建极端轨迹。下一步应把物理源、介质和传感器连成正演计算，明确观察者、距离、尺度、误差与普通竞争解释。

9. Energy, heat and backreaction

9. 能源、热与反作用

A usable system needs a consistent energy and entropy budget through an entire operating cycle. Peak power, average power, energy storage, useful work, heat buffering and sustained heat rejection are different quantities. Landauer's bound applies to specified irreversible erasure; it is not a universal per-operation electricity bill. A cold environment may reduce one cost while making heat rejection or refrigeration more demanding.

The framework includes conditional radiation, water-flow, storage and duty-cycle examples. They establish how assumed loads connect to required resources, not the power of a metric-control device or an observed anomaly. The next step is to attach a chosen model and task to measured material, conversion and transfer parameters, retaining controller losses, maintenance and backreaction in the same boundary.

可用系统需要在整个工作循环中保持能量与熵预算一致。峰值功率、平均功率、储能、有用功、储热和持续排热不是同一个量。Landauer下界适用于规定的不可逆擦除，不能当成所有运算的统一电费。寒冷环境可能降低某项代价，同时增加排热或制冷困难。

框架已有辐射、流水携热、储能与占空比的条件算例，说明给定负载怎样连接资源需求；这些不是度规控制装置功率，也不是异常实测值。下一步将选定模型和任务接到实测材料、转换与传热参数，并把控制损耗、维修和反作用放在同一系统边界内。

10. Information, archives and long-term operation

10. 信息、档案与长期运行

Long-term persistence has several levels: a carrier survives, data remain recoverable, a reader exists, the execution environment remains available, and functioning equipment can be rebuilt. Classical redundancy, quantum error correction and physical repair address different problems. Unknown quantum states cannot be treated as freely clonable files; preserving a preparation recipe is different from preserving an arbitrary state.

The framework specifies sleep, inspection, work and recovery as task-dependent modes and examines common-cause failure and asynchronous backups. It does not demonstrate autonomous repair, indefinite memory life or preservation of consciousness. Next steps require measured loss rates, read/write and maintenance costs, a failure model and a recovery test that ends with calibrated equipment rather than merely a readable archive.

长期保存有多个层次：载体幸存、数据可恢复、读出设备可用、运行环境仍在，以及设备能够重建。经典冗余、量子纠错和实体维修解决不同问题。未知量子态不能视作可随意复制的文件；保存制备说明与保存任意量子态也不相同。

框架按任务划分休眠、巡检、工作和恢复模式，比较共同失效与异步备份。尚未证明自治维修、无限记忆寿命或意识保存。下一步需要实测损失率、读写维修成本、失效模型，以及以恢复到可校准设备为终点的检验，不能止于档案可读。

11. Natural formation, engineering and site selection

11. 自然形成、工程组织与节点选址

Mechanism, origin and organization are separate questions. A natural localized structure may have complex responses without a goal-directed controller; an engineered system may use ordinary physics. Multiple objects might be independent, share environmental forcing or exchange information. Site selection should follow a defined task, response-time requirement and resource budget rather than a list of suggestive locations.

The framework compares local execution, terrestrial archives and off-world redundancy under pressure, corrosion, heat transfer, access and communication constraints. These are conditional design studies, not a detected infrastructure map. The next step is to optimize a minimal task against simpler alternatives and show what observation could distinguish natural structure, engineered operation and actual inter-site communication.

作用机制、来源和组织形式是不同问题。天然局域结构可能有复杂响应而没有目标控制器；人工系统也可以完全使用普通物理。多个对象可能独立、受同一环境驱动，或真正交换信息。选址应从任务、响应时限和资源预算出发，不能从引人联想的地名名单出发。

框架比较本地执行、地球档案与地外冗余所面临的压力、腐蚀、传热、可达性和通信条件。这是条件设计研究，不是发现了设施分布图。下一步要把最小任务与更简单方案比较，并说明什么观测能够区分自然结构、工程运行及真实站间通信。

12. Independent data and failure criteria

12. 独立数据与失败判据

A research loop closes empirically only when a fixed model predicts data not used to adjust it. Reliable inference needs provenance, calibration, correlated-error treatment, selection effects and meaningful held-out units. Adjacent video frames or products sharing the same input are not independent confirmation. A null result constrains a model only where the instrument could have detected its predicted signal.

A synthetic Plummer-versus-polynomial exercise demonstrates overfitting under known data generation, with separate training and test sets. No complete calibrated Tic Tac trajectory, Antarctic inversion or cross-site network detection has been produced. Next steps are ordinary-background modelling, labelled signal injection, blind or held-out recovery, and a parameter-specific exclusion or independently repeated residual that survives competing explanations.

经验闭环要求固定模型预测未用于调参的数据。可靠反演需要来源、标定、相关误差、选择效应和有意义的留出单位。相邻视频帧或共享输入的数据产品不是独立确认。未检出只能在仪器确有能力看见预期信号的区域约束模型。

已运行的合成Plummer模型与多项式比较，用独立训练及测试数据演示过拟合；数据生成规律预先已知。当前没有完成经标定的Tic Tac完整轨迹、南极反演或跨地点网络检出。下一步是普通背景建模、明确标注的信号注入、盲化或留出回收，以及针对具体参数的排除界或可独立重复且经得起竞争解释的残差。

What a closed argument would require

理论闭环的成立条件

The framework distinguishes three kinds of closure. Logical closure means that assumptions and later claims do not contradict one another. Physical closure means that dynamics, sources, boundaries and exchanges of energy, momentum and information are accounted for. Empirical closure means that calibrated, independent observations test a frozen prediction. The existing work offers an organized architecture and several bounded mathematical or numerical checks; it has not completed all three for a new VMAD mechanism.

A defensible advance would be a model-specific reconstruction condition, an obstruction to a proposed local transition, a verified response law, or an independently testable exclusion region. The polynomial tidal potential is used only over the localized source region; no global equilibrium with the finite driving masses is claimed.

Specified driving source. Two ordinary masses, each 0.5M at ±200a on the z axis, generate η = 1.25×10⁻⁷ in the leading local quadrupole. Common observables and source degeneracy. For the stated static weak field, the outer-minus-inner fractional clock rate is [Φ(out)−Φ(in)]/c² and the radial tidal coefficient is Γrr = −∂²rΦ. Identical Newtonian density profiles produce identical clock and tidal predictions. Agreement between channels therefore checks common geometry but does not uniquely identify a scalar source. Additional independent source constraints or a separately fixed coupling are needed.

An identity term a(τ)I in the isolated SP evolution changes only its global phase and has zero density and Newtonian-potential response. This latter reference-shift control does not model an actuator's stress-energy. Neither control is a claim that all physical quantum operations leave relativistic gravity unchanged.

Six-month research programme

六个月研究计划

The six-month access-period objective is one necessary link in the proposed chain, not a complete theory of emergent matter, local spacetime dissolution and metric control. Entropy-based spatial reconstruction is the initial candidate route. One model, state family, map and testable claim will be fixed before extension.

MonthWorkReviewable output
1Select one published model and map; fix a claim, dynamics and approximation; personally review a baseline.Model specification, prior-work comparison and recorded applicant review.
2Reproduce one relevant reconstruction result; define observables, partitions and validity criteria.Auditable derivation and a fixed reference calculation.
3Apply one admissible local change; vary coarse-graining and numerical resolution.Conditions for preserved, altered or inadequate geometric reconstruction.
4Separate physical change from noise or missing information; examine finite-size and dynamical limitations.A conditional result, counterexample or unresolved condition; no phase claim from a finite diagnostic alone.
5Audit the required bridge to local transitions and control: coupling, boundaries, conservation, causality and resources.Supported bounds where derivable, otherwise explicit missing conditions; conventional gravity kept as a separate reference.
6Consolidate derivations, adverse cases and reproducible checks.Model-specific theoretical report and the next independently testable step.

The minimum deliverable is one precisely scoped claim with its assumptions, derivation or counterexample, reproducible check and remaining gap. A model that fails the geometric criterion is a reportable outcome. Strict proofs will be pursued where tractable. Neither an embedding nor a changing entropy curve alone establishes physical spacetime or a phase transition. Earth-environment applications require separate empirical validation.

首期目标是拟议链条中的一项必要连接，不是在六个月内完成物质涌现、局部时空消亡与度规控制的完整理论。基于熵的空间重建是初始候选路线；延伸之前先固定一个模型、态族、映射和可检验命题。

月份工作可审阅交付
第1月选定一个已发表模型和映射，固定命题、动力学与近似，本人复核一项基准。模型说明、前人工作对照及本人复核记录。
第2月复现一项相关重建结果，定义观测量、分区及有效判据。可审核推导与固定参照计算。
第3月施加一种允许的局部变化，比较粗粒化及数值分辨率。保持、改变或不足以支持几何重建的条件。
第4月区分物理变化、噪声与信息不足，审查有限尺寸及动力学限制。条件结论、反例或未解决条件；不由有限诊断直接宣称相变。
第5月审查通向局部相变与控制的桥接要求：耦合、边界、守恒、因果与资源。能推导时给出界限，否则明确缺失条件；常规引力另作对照。
第6月整理推导、不利案例和可复现检查。限定模型理论报告及下一项可独立检验步骤。

最低交付是一项范围准确的命题，附假设、推导或反例、可复现检查及剩余缺口。模型不能满足几何判据也属于可报告结果。可处理的主张争取严格证明；嵌入图或熵曲线改变本身，不足以确立物理时空或相变。与地球环境对应，仍需独立经验检验。

Later observational and environmental extensions

后续观测与环境延伸

Tic Tac is a later observational test case, not the foundation of the microscopic theory. The records must first be separated by provenance, platform and sensor, then tested for target identity, range, timing and motion. A video centroid, witness account and a reported radar track cannot simply be combined into one extreme trajectory. Any proposed geometric mechanism must predict additional measurements and outperform ordinary propagation, tracking, environmental and source alternatives.

Deep-ocean and Antarctic environments enter through defined tasks and measurable backgrounds. Ocean water can transport heat but brings pressure, corrosion and communication costs; ice and rock may offer archive conditions but also motion, melt, conduction and access problems. Multi-physics work would have to account for ordinary geology and shared inputs between gravity, topography and ice products. No site is presently identified as a VMAD installation.

The Moon and icy satellites extend the comparison to redundancy, low-background observations, ocean science and recovery resources. Lunar far-side radio shielding, polar cold traps and a communication relay are distinct properties. A network requires independently supported nodes, a common parameter set and new timed predictions that distinguish communication from a shared driver. Finite propagation time prevents a distant archive from supplying instantaneous local control. A branch is weakened when its predicted signal should be detectable but is absent, when ordinary explanations succeed, or when its task is better achieved by a simpler configuration.

Tic Tac属于后续观测案例，不是微观理论的基础。必须先按来源、平台和传感器拆开记录，再检验目标身份、距离、时间与运动。不能把视频像斑、目击陈述和转述雷达轨迹直接拼成同一条极端轨迹。候选几何机制需要预测额外观测，并优于普通传播、跟踪、环境与物理源解释。

海底与南极通过指定任务和可测背景进入研究。海水可以携热，也带来压力、腐蚀和通信代价；冰岩可能提供档案条件，同时存在流动、融化、传导和维护问题。多物理研究需要纳入普通地质机制，以及重力、地形和冰产品之间共享的输入。目前没有任何地点被识别为VMAD设施。

月球与冰卫星把比较扩展到冗余、低背景观测、海洋科学与恢复资源。月背射频屏蔽、极区冷阱和通信中继是不同条件。网络证据要求独立支持的节点、共同参数，以及能区分通信和共同驱动的新时序预测。有限传播时间不允许远方档案承担瞬时本地控制。若预测本应可检出却未见、普通解释成功，或更简单配置更好完成任务，相应分支就应收缩或撤回。
